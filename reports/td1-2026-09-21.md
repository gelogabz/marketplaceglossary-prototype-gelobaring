## Suggested (score 2, optional)

#### Multi-Account AWS Marketplace Management — Suger (new_term)
- Organizations can now connect multiple AWS Marketplace seller accounts inside Suger, choosing a default and picking a specific account per listed product. Distinct from AWS Partner Central's own "linked accounts" feature (already a separate term) — this is Suger's own console-side account-scoping model for products/offers/entitlements.
- Source: https://www.suger.io/resources/changelog/ (Sept 14, 2026 entry) — also confirmed via `whats-new.js`.

#### Co-sell Email Actions — Suger (new_term)
- Referral emails now carry one-click action buttons (Accept, Mark Closed Won/Lost, Approve Commission) that expire after 24 hours and are single-use. Can send from a user's own connected Gmail account.
- Source: https://www.suger.io/resources/changelog/ (Sept 14, 2026 entry)

#### Revenue (updated_term)
- The Revenue dashboard was restructured around four summary cards: contract value, channel-by-channel deferred balance rollover, 12-month recognition forecast, and pending revenue-record queue. Current def describes the dashboard generically — worth reflecting the new structure.
- Source: https://www.suger.io/resources/changelog/ (Sept 14, 2026 entry)
